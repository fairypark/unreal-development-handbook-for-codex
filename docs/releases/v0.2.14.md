# v0.2.14 — Korean review artifacts and verifiable blockout promotion

This release makes user-review artifacts accessible in Korean across every
bundled Skill and strengthens the evidence required to promote an Unreal level
from an approved spatial plan through blockout and into the visual feasibility
slice. It preserves the risk-based Stage 2 vertical-information policy from
v0.2.13 while adding explicit spatial locks, independent review, and visual
retirement rules for blockout primitives.

## Included

- Added a common Korean review-artifact contract to every bundled Skill. When
  a decision-bearing artifact is not Korean, the workflow now requires a
  separately preserved Korean version with equivalent decision content.
- Required Korean localization inside decision-bearing visuals, including
  labels, annotations, legends, captions, and callouts. Text-free visuals must
  be paired with a Korean review sheet containing the visual, review criteria,
  and decision notes.
- Added a post-design blank-level observation baseline. Lighting, sky,
  atmosphere, fog, exposure, and post-process mutation remain locked until the
  concept, Area Composition Plan, translation contract, and applicable asset
  plan pass.
- Made the exact approved floor-plan version a spatial mutation lock during
  blockout. Every content-bearing batch must trace to stable plan features and
  tolerances, then compare realized transforms, bounds, routes, terrain,
  required voids, and relationships before the next batch.
- Added an independent read-only blockout reference-fidelity review separate
  from the builder. Stage 6 remains locked until the exact candidate passes
  same-condition concept-art, floor-plan, overview, runtime-camera, and
  plan-conformance review.
- Added explicit non-convergence handling. Failed candidates and evidence are
  preserved, one major variable changes per repair, material delays are
  reported immediately, and possible source-design problems reopen the
  responsible design gates instead of weakening the acceptance bar.
- Added a blockout-cube visual-retirement gate. Every cube or primitive must be
  `REPLACE`, `RETIRE`, or `RETAIN_INTERNAL_SKELETON`; retained skeletons remain
  fully enclosed or non-rendering and cannot gain production visual authority
  through material assignment.
- Added regression coverage for Korean review artifacts, blank-level lighting
  order, floor-plan spatial locking, independent blockout supervision, and
  blockout-cube retirement.
- Ignored generated Codex environment metadata and Python bytecode caches so
  local validation does not pollute the release worktree.

## Compatibility with v0.2.13

The Stage 2 Area Composition Plan remains a versioned package whose plan-view
functions are inspected before a recorded `vertical_information_assessment`.
Named side or elevation profiles are required only when the vertical evidence
is insufficient. The new floor-plan spatial lock begins after that plan package
and the Stage 2a translation gate pass; it does not force redundant view files
or weaken the risk-based evidence decision.
