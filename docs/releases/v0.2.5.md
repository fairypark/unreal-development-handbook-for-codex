# v0.2.5 — Stage-aware level camera validation

This patch release defines which camera evidence may approve each level-production decision without weakening the existing staged workflow or overview-camera restrictions.

## Included

- Area Composition Plans and terrain/macro blockouts now require a risk-covering set of fixed overview cameras with stable IDs for arrival, reverse, lateral, waterway/axis, elevation, and project-specific relationships; one hero overview or a universal camera count is insufficient.
- Every overview remains `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`. Overview evidence may gate assigned macro-composition relationships but cannot approve player visibility, scale, readability, landmark reading, or typology.
- Static cameras at the recorded player height and FOV are limited to rejecting gross building-scale, route-width, slope, and occlusion failures during Stages 2–4; they do not authorize detailed player-view composition or runtime-camera behavior.
- Stage 5 Playable Blockout now explicitly gives the representative player controller and actual runtime camera rig authority over camera-dependent scale, visibility, readability, and typology evidence, including relevant boom, offset, pitch, SpringArm collision, and retraction behavior.
- Golden/Visual Feasibility Slice evidence now judges player readability, asset density, and visual composition through the Stage 5-approved runtime rig.
- The World & Level Design and Validation Skills, Development Process chapter, and Validation, Testing & Debugging chapter use the same camera-authority model.
