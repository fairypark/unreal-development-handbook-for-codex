# v0.2.6 — Dependent-strata strategy gate for procedural dressing

This release makes AI consider and record a placement strategy before generating spatially dependent strata such as rocks or hardscape followed by grass or ground cover.

## Included

- Added the conditional `Dependent-Strata Strategy Gate` before the first relevant PCG, Foliage, or batch-placement mutation.
- Added explicit `VIDEO_DISTANCE_EXCLUSION`, `MASK_OTHER`, `DIRECT_AUTHORED`, `PENDING_EVIDENCE`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE` decisions with reasons, source authority, dependency order, units, clearance, transition band, and validation status.
- Added the `dependent_strata_strategy` field to the Stage 2a Reference-to-Prototype Translation Contract and bumped its schema to `1.1.0`.
- Added semantic validation that broad placement cannot pass while an applicable dependent-strata strategy is missing or pending.
- Clarified the rock-footprint/ground-cover exclusion pattern, final transformed mesh-gap validation, and Nanite versus distance-culling evidence requirements.
