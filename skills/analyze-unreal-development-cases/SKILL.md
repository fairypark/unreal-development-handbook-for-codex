# Analyze Unreal Development Cases

Reconstruct the decision and its evidence before extracting lessons. Treat the case as applied reasoning, not a recipe.

## Load the Handbook chapter

Read [16-case-studies.md](references/16-case-studies.md) before admitting, structuring, or generalizing a case. Keep candidate cases explicitly pending when source evidence is incomplete.

## Reconstruct the case

Record:

1. original intent, stakeholders, and desired outcome;
2. project context, constraints, assumptions, and success criteria;
3. architecture and content state before the change;
4. alternatives considered and why they were accepted or rejected;
5. selected decision and implementation scope;
6. validation evidence, production outcome, and side effects;
7. failures, recovery actions, regressions, and unresolved risks.

Separate observed facts from interpretation. Do not infer causality from sequence alone, reward effort as quality, or hide missing evidence.

## Extract durable lessons

For each lesson, explain:

- the visible or measurable symptom;
- the likely cause and confidence level;
- the decision rule that would prevent or detect recurrence;
- contexts where the lesson applies;
- contexts where it should not be generalized;
- related architecture, level, content, implementation, performance, validation, production, or AI topics.

Remove private paths, credentials, user data, exact coordinates, proprietary asset names, and accidental project details unless they are essential and authorized. Preserve enough context to understand the trade-off.

Conclude with transferable decisions, evidence gaps, and follow-up research. Do not turn a single anecdote into a universal best practice.
